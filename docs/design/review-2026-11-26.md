# Design & UX review: 2026-11-26

Impeccable v4.11.1 (engine 0.1.31) review of https://project-yukikaki.vercel.app and the dev server, followed by a streamline of the landing page and site language. DESIGN.md is new in this pass and is now the design source of truth.

**Method.** `impeccable detect` on `app/` + `components/` (static) and on rendered pages: `/en`, `/ja`, `/en/events`, an event (`sapporo-snow-tech-hackathon-2027`), `/en/challenges`, a challenge, `/en/grants`, `/en/interview` (redirects to sign-in when signed out) and the 404. Rendered passes at 1440×900 light, 1440×900 dark and 390×844 light. Manual UX/CX walk in a real browser at 1440×900 and 390×844, light and dark, EN and JA, along four journeys: an owner brings a problem, a builder finds and joins an event, a host reviews, a visitor checks grants. Checklists from `.github/agents/impeccable-finish-reviewer.agent.md` (evidence, floor, truth) and `impeccable-motion-reviewer.agent.md` (craft, reduced motion) were applied in-thread; this run had no sub-agent tool, so their filmstrip/comp inputs were not produced.

**Note on URL scans.** `impeccable detect <url>` times out on every page of this site: Clerk's bot-protection probes (`*.client.protect.clerk.com:8443/…/probe`) never finish, so the page never reaches network idle. Rendered scans therefore load the page in Playwright Chromium and run `impeccable detect --cdp`. `pnpm design:check:live` does this for you.

## Findings by severity

**P1 (fix before release)**
1. **Raw data keys as copy** on challenges list, challenge detail, event detail and the filter selects: "Setagaya · bakery", "snow_removal", "data-analysis", even on JA pages. Hardcoded bilingual badges ("合成 / Synthetic"). *Fixed:* `cityLabel`, `industryLabel`, `common.skills.*`, `common.synthetic`.
2. **Dark-mode contrast on inner pages:** `text-slate-500` (#62748e) on #08131f/#0f172b = 3.7–3.9:1 across challenges, challenge detail, grants, admin, me, teams, onboarding and event form (37 places). *Fixed:* `text-muted` token (adapts per theme).
3. **Search placeholder contrast** on /challenges: 2.6:1 (`slate-400` on white). *Fixed:* `slate-500` light / `slate-400` dark (≥4.5:1).
4. **Marquee without a pause control** (WCAG 2.2.2): hover/focus paused it, touch users could not. *Fixed:* "Pause scrolling" toggle (hidden under reduced motion, where the marquee is already a static row).
5. **Hero "Coming soon" teaser covered the shoveller** (the brand's protagonist) at 1024, 1280, 1440 and 1920 wide. *Fixed:* anchored to `bottom: calc(9.5vw + 1.5rem)`, which clears the kid at every width.
6. **Hero quote bar:** byline 3.2:1 (EN) / 3.5:1 (JA) over the snowfield; JA quote set in faux italic. *Fixed:* stronger night scrim, byline at full `sky-100`, upright JA.
7. **Frost text on night** in the party-builder preview label: 3.5:1. *Fixed:* `sky-200` (the label was later removed in the streamline).
8. **Signed-out builder dead end** on challenge detail: no way to join. *Fixed:* "Sign in as a builder to form a team" with a return URL.
9. **Sign-in wall says nothing about why** ("Welcome back!" to a first-time owner from "Bring a problem"). *Fixed:* one line above the Clerk card on sign-in and sign-up; Clerk themed to night navy.

**P2**
10. **Off-palette event covers** (violet/fuchsia, orange/rose): read as another product. *Fixed:* frost/ice/lantern over night navy.
11. **Text under 11px**: "Coming soon" chip, party tags, avatar initials (10px). *Fixed:* 11px floor (now a DESIGN.md rule).
12. **Bounce easing** on the active kitchen-quest hotspot (`kq-bounce`, steps(2) jump). *Fixed:* `kq-lift`, `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` alternate (exponential ease-out); still off under reduced motion.
13. **Eyebrows/kickers above 9 headings** (landing ×8, events page). *Fixed:* removed (craft floor ban). Hero tagline line kept as the brand line.
14. **"0 h/week" proof stat** when no evaluations exist reads as anti-proof. *Fixed:* stat renders only when hours > 0.
15. **Emoji as icon** in the challenges search field (🔎). *Fixed:* lucide `Search`. Emoji tiles in "Who wins" went with that section.
16. **Header nav up to 6 items for hosts**; "Get help" did not say what it does. *Fixed:* Events, Challenges, Grants, Bring a problem / My page, Admin. Graph moved to a link on /admin.

**P3**
17. Value-strip pill text centered beside its bullet when it wraps on phones. *Fixed:* `text-left`.
18. Long source snippets on challenge detail ran ~90 characters per line. *Fixed:* `max-w-prose`.

**False positives / not ours.** Clerk footer contrast in dark mode (the card is light; the detector sampled the page behind it). Clerk "Development mode" label (dev keys only). The giant footer wordmark at 1.2:1 is `aria-hidden` decoration.

## Streamline: landing story and language

**Section order, before (15 blocks):** hero (eyebrow, wordmark, h2, subtitle, positioning, value strip, quick filters, CTAs, teaser, quote bar) → needs block (lead + paragraph + Hokkaido line) → challenges marquee (kicker) → hours stat → How it works (kicker, 6 workstreams, approval note) → Kitchen quest (kicker) → Formats harness (kicker) → Principles (kicker, 6 cards) → Who wins (kicker, 4 emoji cards) → Roadmap + party builder (2 kickers) → Testimonials (kicker, up to 6) → Closing (kicker line).

**Section order, after (one story):**
1. **What it is:** hero. Tagline line, wordmark, h2 "Your test kitchen for ideas and prototypes.", one subtitle, search, three value pills, Bring a problem / For hosts, AI-teammates teaser, floating testimonial.
2. **Why it matters:** "Most local ideas stall before anyone builds." plus two sentences (merges the needs block, the Hokkaido lead line, How-it-works' lead and Principles' intent).
3. **How it works (shown, not listed):** Kitchen quest, then Formats harness ("Same prep, any format"). The harness's guardrail row now carries the principles.
4. **Proof:** live challenges marquee (with pause), hours given back (only when > 0), two owner quotes.
5. **What's coming:** roadmap, party-builder preview, one waitlist.
6. **Act:** "Fewer hands, same town." with Bring a problem / For hosts.

**Cut or merged:** How it works (6 workstreams + approval note; the kitchen quest shows the same steps), Principles (6 cards; kept as the harness guardrails), Who wins (4 cards; audiences are now addressed by the CTAs and the roadmap), the positioning paragraph (jargon: "Peatix or Luma for hackathons"), every kicker, the closing pre-heading, testimonials beyond two, the "Your test kitchen…" duplicate in the footer story.

**Fixed terms** (also in DESIGN.md): **challenge** (not brief; JA 課題/チャレンジ), **owner** (事業者/店主), **builder** (ビルダー; never "student" in UI copy, the `student` role key stays), **host** (主催者; replaces 自治体の担当者/ホスト/まちの担当者 in UI). "Bring a problem" (課題を相談) is the owner's action everywhere: nav, hero, closing, mobile bar.

**e2e.** `e2e/flow.spec.ts` asserts no string changed here (h2, "チームを組んで挑戦する", "はじめる", "送信する" and the admin buttons are unchanged). Its banner assertions (`"Your test kitchen for hackathons."`, `"ハッカソンのための、まちのテストキッチン。"`) already did not match the header tagline before this pass; they were left alone (out of scope, and changing them is not ours to decide).

## Open recommendations

1. **Tokenize the shared UI kit** (`components/ui.tsx`): `Card`, `Button`, `Badge`, `PageHeader` still use raw slate/white. Map to `surface`/`line`/`ink`/`muted`, and make the primary button lantern (one lantern per region). Inner pages would then match the landing. `/impeccable polish`
2. **Admin and team pages** were not reviewed signed in (no host session in this run). Walk the host journey with a host account next month.
3. **Avatar initials** overlap and clip at `-space-x-1.5`; increase spacing or use a single "+N going" pill. `/impeccable layout`
4. **Mobile hero search placeholder** truncates mid-word at 390 ("…by town, ir"). Shorten for small screens ("Search by town or skill…"). `/impeccable clarify`
5. **Quote bar covers the shoveller at 1024–1280 wide**; consider offsetting the bar to the right of the kid on mid widths (constraint: the bar stays).
6. **Clerk sign-in/up** could be fully localized in tone ("Welcome back!" is Clerk's), via Clerk localization overrides.
7. **`.impeccable/design.json` sidecar** (component snippets for the live panel) was not generated; run `/impeccable document` to add it.
8. **e2e banner assertions** in `e2e/flow.spec.ts` (lines 42 and 54) expect an old tagline; update them to `nav.tagline` when someone owns the e2e suite.

## CX feedback micro-survey (recommendation, not built)

Six questions, under a minute, sent after an event (attendees and builders) and after delivery (owners), EN/JA. Reuse the feedback-token flow in `app/[locale]/feedback/`.

1. How easy was it to find what you needed on Yukikaki? (1–5)
2. Did you know what to do next at every step? (Yes / Mostly / No; if not, where did you get stuck?)
3. *Owners:* Did the challenge we wrote describe your problem correctly? *Builders:* Did you have what you needed (problem, data, owner contact) from the first hour? (1–5)
4. Was anything unclear or confusing in the wording, in English or Japanese? (free text)
5. How likely are you to bring another problem / join another event? (0–10)
6. One thing we should change before the next event? (free text)

Report it monthly next to the design review: question 2 and 4 answers map directly to `/impeccable clarify` and `/impeccable onboard` work.
